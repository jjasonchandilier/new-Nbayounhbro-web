Beyond

NBAYOUNBRO84 is five brothers bound not by blood but by frequency. Born from the same hunger to create the uncreatable, we craft music that lives at the edge of imagination — where every beat is a new universe.

Our sound is impossible to categorise. Infectious hooks forged in midnight sessions. Melodies that burrow deep and never leave. Lyricism that paints pictures words were never designed to paint.

We are Diezl, Vedanth, Adam, Jarrah, and Pao — and we are just getting started.
